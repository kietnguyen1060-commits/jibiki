# 字引 Jibiki

A self-study toolkit for Japanese that works alongside Anki. The interface and study content are in Vietnamese.

- [`kanji-app/`](kanji-app/) — a local web app: draw a character on a whiteboard to look it up, open cards in the style of Kanji Look and Learn (component pictures, stroke order, example words), and study directly from your Anki collection through AnkiConnect.
- Text scan shows the meaning, Sino-Vietnamese reading and pronunciation of any selected text, and the app has built-in daily notes.
- [`minna-n5/`](minna-n5/) — 5 N5 tests, each combining 5 lessons of Minna no Nihongo I, covering all the vocabulary and grammar, with a test timer and score history.
- [`minna-n4/`](minna-n4/) — 25 N4 tests, one for each lesson of Minna no Nihongo II (26–50), covering grammar, vocabulary and kanji.
- Practice pages for N5 (`#luyen-1`…`#luyen-25`) and N4 (`#luyen-26`…`#luyen-50`): every word and kanji in a lesson is asked (reading and writing), plus grammar questions for patterns the tests rarely cover and reading passages that require inference. N4 also has word usage questions. Original questions live in `data/luyen-*.json`, and the drills are generated by `tools/luyen.py`. Nothing is copied from exam prep books.
- N5–N4 grammar comparison (`minna-n4/data/so-sanh-n5-n4.json`, the "So sánh N5–N4" tab on both pages): 16 groups of easily confused patterns, with how they differ, when to use each, and tips for choosing.

## Data not included in this repo

Book content (the mnemonics and examples from Kanji Look and Learn, the Minna no Nihongo vocabulary lists) was extracted from a personal Anki deck, so it is not published here. After cloning:

1. `kanji-app/app/data/app-data.js`: run `kanji-app/tools/build_data.py` on a copy of your `collection.anki2` (see [kanji-app/README.md](kanji-app/README.md)).
2. `minna-n5/data/vocab.json`, `minna-n4/data/vocab.json`: vocabulary lists by lesson (see `tools/check.py` in each folder for the required fields). Once you have them, run `tools/build.py` in each folder to create the test pages in `dist/`, then copy them to `kanji-app/app/minna/n5.html` and `n4.html`.

The tests, grammar explanations, 392 additional kanji mnemonics (`kanji-app/tools/extra_vi_*.txt`) and component pictures are original work in this repo.
